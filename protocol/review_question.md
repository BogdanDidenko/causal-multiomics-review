# Review Question

## Primary Question

Which empirical studies integrate multiple omics layers to evaluate a
biological or health-related causal claim, and what source of identification
supports that claim?

## PCC Framing

- **Population:** humans, animals, cells, tissues, organoids, microorganisms,
  or other biological systems relevant to health or biological mechanisms.
- **Concept:** measured or jointly integrated multi-omics data used within an
  assessable causal design.
- **Context:** primary research published in English from 2018 onward,
  including peer-reviewed articles and preprints.

## Design Families

- genetic instrument / Mendelian randomization / SMR-HEIDI;
- randomized intervention;
- non-randomized or quasi-experimental intervention;
- direct perturbation such as CRISPR or Perturb-seq;
- temporal or longitudinal identification;
- formal causal mediation;
- graphical, structural-equation, Bayesian-network, or directed model with an
  assessable identification argument.

The labels used by authors are not eligibility criteria. A study may be
relevant without saying `causal inference` or `causal discovery`, and may be
ineligible despite using those phrases.
