# Eligibility Criteria

## Inclusion Criteria

| Code | Criterion | Title/abstract handling |
|---|---|---|
| IC1 | Primary empirical biological or health research. | `unclear` advances. |
| IC2 | At least two omics layers are measured or jointly integrated in the analyzed study data. | Mere mention of external omics evidence is insufficient. |
| IC3 | The report contains an assessable causal design or direct perturbation relevant to a biological claim. | Design-specific evidence is accepted even without causal terminology. |
| IC4 | Publication date is 2018-01-01 or later and the report is in English. | Enforced from source metadata where possible. |
| IC5 | Article or preprint provides enough report-level information for full-text assessment. | Insufficient abstracts remain `unclear`. |

## Exclusion Codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EC1 | Not primary empirical research: review, editorial, commentary, correction, or protocol. |
| EC2 | Method, benchmark, software, or data resource without an eligible empirical analysis. |
| EC3 | Does not measure or jointly integrate at least two omics layers. |
| EC4 | Not biological or health research. |
| EC5 | No assessable causal design; association, prediction, or causal wording only. |
| EC6 | Outside date or language limits. |
| EC7 | Duplicate report or superseded preprint. |
| EC8 | Full text unavailable after documented retrieval attempts. |

## Operational Definitions

`Multi-omics` requires at least two molecular layers, such as genomics,
epigenomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, lipidomics, or
microbiomics. Multiple assays from one layer do not automatically qualify.

`Assessable causal design` means the report identifies an intervention,
instrument, temporal/structural restriction, mediator estimand, perturbation,
or directed model whose assumptions can be examined. A causal adjective,
directional edge, pathway enrichment, or feature-importance ranking alone does
not satisfy IC3.
